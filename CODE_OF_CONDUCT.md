# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or technology choice.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- Show empathy and kindness toward others

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Personal attacks, demeaning language, or threats
- Posting or sharing others' private information without consent
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or events
- Submission of low-quality, spammy, or irrelevant resources

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a GitHub issue in this repository. All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take the following actions for violations:

- Removal of submitted resources
- Comment moderation
- Temporary or permanent ban from contributing

This Code of Conduct applies to all WebAtlas spaces, including GitHub repositories, discussions, and any events organized by the community.
